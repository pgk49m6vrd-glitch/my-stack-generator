## 2026-03-05 - Structural Active Navigation States
**Learning:** Relying solely on text color or font-weight changes for active navigation links (e.g., `<NavLink>`) fails WCAG 1.4.1 (Use of Color), disadvantaging users who cannot perceive the color difference.
**Action:** Always provide a structural layout change, such as a background color pill or a border indicator, in addition to color changes, to ensure accessible visual distinction for the active state.
